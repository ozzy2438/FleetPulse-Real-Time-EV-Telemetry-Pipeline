About the job
Ready to take the next step in your Ventia career? 

Ventia delivers complex transport infrastructure and technology solutions across Victoria’s major road network. The VIMC contract involves the operation, maintenance and performance management of critical Intelligent Transport Systems, traffic signal assets, communications networks, electrical infrastructure and associated roadside technology across live traffic environments.

The maintenance workforce plays a critical role in keeping the road network safe, reliable and operational for the traveling public. Works are performed across metropolitan and regional Victoria and include planned maintenance, fault response, emergency works, inspections, repairs, testing and restoration of traffic signals and ITS assets.

About The Role

The Data Analyst is responsible for extracting, analyzing and presenting asset, maintenance, fault, performance and operational data to support effective contract delivery. Working closely with operations, planning, asset management, commercial and compliance teams, you will transform complex datasets into meaningful insights that support decision making, contract performance and continuous improvement. This is an exciting opportunity for a curious and technically capable analyst who enjoys identifying trends, solving problems and delivering data-driven outcomes.

Role Responsibilities

Extract, analyse and interpret maintenance, asset and operational data from a range of systems and reporting platforms. 
Develop dashboards, reports and analytical insights to support contract performance, asset reliability and operational decision-making. 
Monitor and analyse KPI performance, response times, rectification performance and workload trends. 
Identify patterns, anomalies, emerging risks and opportunities for improvement across asset and operational data. 
Support forecasting, resource planning and workload management through data-driven analysis. 
Assist with report automation, data quality initiatives and continuous improvement of reporting tools and processes. 
Provide analytical support for audits, contract reviews, commercial assessments and improvement initiatives. 

About You

Experience in data analysis, business intelligence, reporting or performance analytics roles. 
Advanced Excel and Power BI skills, with the ability to present complex information in a clear and meaningful way. 
Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving capability and attention to detail. 
Experience working with large datasets and reporting tools such as Maximo, SQL, Python or similar platforms is highly regarded. 
Ability to communicate insights effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. 
Experience within infrastructure, maintenance, transport, utilities or other asset-intensive environments is desirable. 
Relevant qualifications in Data Analytics, Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics, Business, Asset Management or a related discipline are desirable.
